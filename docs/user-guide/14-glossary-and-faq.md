# 14. Glossary and FAQ

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ELC** | Employee Life Cycle: the whole journey from candidate to exit. |
| **PnC / P&C** | People & Culture, NavGurukul's HR team. |
| **Core** | The main platform database. Holds everything except the most sensitive data. |
| **Vault** | The separate, locked store for salary, bank, ID numbers and written feedback. Separate login; the tech team can't read it. |
| **Sealing** | Moving a document or feedback from the platform into the vault once the work on it is done. |
| **Role** | What you're allowed to see and do. Each person has one. |
| **Role View Mode** | Lets an admin see the site as a lower role would. |
| **Candidate** | Someone with an offer who hasn't accepted yet. |
| **Pre-joiner** | Someone who accepted and is completing documents before Day 1. |
| **Portal / portal link** | The candidate's personal onboarding page, opened from their invitation email. No login. |
| **Dossier** | Everything a candidate submits: details, documents, policy reads, signed letters. |
| **Provisioning / Create Official Account** | Turning an approved candidate into an employee with an official email and employee code. |
| **Annexure A** | The salary breakdown attached to the offer. |
| **CTC** | Cost to Company: the total annual compensation agreed in the offer. |
| **Signatory** | The person named as signing authority on letters. |
| **FTE / FTC** | Full-time employee / full-time consultant (on this platform). |
| **Confirmation** | The end-of-probation review that decides whether someone is confirmed. |
| **Appraisal** | The twice-yearly performance review (July and December cycles). |
| **Cohort** | The group of people who fall into one appraisal cycle. |
| **Self-reflection** | The employee's own form in a review. |
| **Peer review** | Feedback from colleagues the employee nominated. Anonymous to the employee. |
| **Not responding** | Someone who hasn't answered after the set number of reminders. PnC is told. |
| **LWD** | Last working day. |
| **NDC** | No Dues Certificate: sign-off from Manager, IT, Finance and PnC before an exit closes. |
| **F&F** | Full-and-final settlement: the leaver's final pay calculation and payment. |
| **UTR** | The bank's reference number for a payment. |
| **STUB** | NavGurukul's attendance system. |
| **Maker / checker / approver** | The three different people a payroll run must pass through (planned). |

## FAQ

**I can't sign in.**
Use your **@navgurukul.org** Google account. Personal accounts are refused.

**A page sent me back to the Dashboard.**
Your role doesn't include that page. If you need it for your work, ask your
team's admin. See [Roles and access](02-roles-and-access.md).

**My Profile says "Employee Record Not Linked".**
Your login email doesn't match your employee record. Ask PnC to correct the
email on your record.

**I don't see My Team.**
It only shows when at least one person has you as their reporting manager on
record. Ask PnC to check the reporting lines.

**I'm a manager and I can't open Confirmations.**
That's expected for most managers. Work from **My Reviews → My team's
reviews**.

**Can the employee see what I wrote in their review?**
No. They see who has responded, not what anyone wrote, and not the decision.
Peer feedback is shown to the manager and PnC without names.

**I nominated the wrong peer.**
Once your self-reflection is submitted, nominations lock. Ask PnC to add or
withdraw a peer.

**The meeting time doesn't work.**
Open the review and use **Reschedule and re-send invite**. The invite updates
for everyone.

**A candidate says they didn't get the invitation.**
Check **Email Center › Delivery Monitor & Outbox**. Then use **Resend
Invitation Email** on the candidate, or copy their portal link and send it
yourself. (*Resend* in the outbox doesn't send anything.)

**Why can't PnC Ops make an offer?**
Making an offer means entering the CTC, which is salary data. Only PnC Admin
and Super Admin can do that.

**Why can't Finance see employees' addresses or ID numbers?**
The access rules give personal and identity details to the employee and PnC
only. Finance sees what payroll and settlements need.

**Can I undo an employee import?**
No. Always check the preview before clicking **Import**.

**Where is payroll?**
Not built yet. See the [Finance guide](11-finance-guide.md#payroll-whats-planned).

**Who do I ask about…**

| Topic | Ask |
|---|---|
| Access, roles, employee records, onboarding, reviews, exits | PnC Admin |
| Finance roles, F&F, salary | Fin Admin |
| Settings, email problems, forms, something broken | Platform Admin |
