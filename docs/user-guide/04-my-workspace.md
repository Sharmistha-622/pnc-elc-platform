# 4. My Workspace

These screens are about **you**. Every staff member has them.

## Dashboard (`/`)

Your home page. It opens with a greeting and a one-line focus for your role,
followed by two parts.

### Waiting on you

Your to-do list. When it's empty you see *"You're all caught up — nothing is
waiting on you."*

| List | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Forms to fill in** | Forms you haven't started or haven't finished. *View all* opens My Forms. |
| **Feedback you've been asked for** | Peer feedback someone has nominated you for. |
| **Decisions for your team** | "Confirm *name*": a probation decision for someone who reports to you. Only shown to roles that can open Confirmations. |

### At a glance

Number tiles. Click a tile to open the screen where that work is done. Each
role sees different tiles:

| Role | Tiles |
|---|---|
| Super Admin | Active employees · In the pipeline · Open confirmations · Exits in progress · Failed emails · Settings changes |
| PnC Admin | Documents to verify · Joining in 14 days · Open confirmations · Exits in progress · Active employees |
| PnC Ops | Documents to verify · Joining in 14 days · Feedback to chase · Exits in progress |
| Fin Admin | Joined this month · Leaving this month · Settlements due · Active employees |
| Fin Ops | Joined this month · Leaving this month · Active employees |
| Exec View | Active employees · Joined this month · Leaving this month · Open confirmations |
| Platform Admin | Failed emails · Settings changes (last 7 days) |
| Manager | Direct reports · On probation |
| Employee, Platform Ops | *(no tiles)* |

## My Profile (`/profile`)

Your employment record, **read-only**:

- Name, role, employment type, email, team.
- **Employment Status**, **Department**, **Joining Date**.
- **Confirmation Status**: *Confirmed (date)* or *In Probation*.
- **Reporting Manager**: who you report to. Only PnC (PnC Admin, PnC Ops,
  Super Admin) can change it.
- **Direct Reportees**: the people who report to you, if any.

To correct anything (phone, address, emergency contact, a wrong manager),
ask PnC. Employees can't edit their own details on the platform yet.

If you see **"Employee Record Not Linked"**, your Google login doesn't match
the email on your employee record. Ask PnC to fix the email on your record.

## My Forms (`/my-forms`)

Every form you've been asked to fill in: your self-reflection, peer feedback
for a colleague, a manager review for your report, or any other form PnC has
sent you.

- Tiles: **Total Forms**, **To Complete**, **In Progress**, **Submitted**.
- Search, and filter by status (*Not Started, In Progress, Submitted, Closed*).
- Click **Open**, **Continue** or **View**.

Inside a form:

- Fields marked **\*** are required when you submit.
- Some questions appear only after you answer an earlier one.
- **Save draft** keeps your answers so you can come back later.
- **Submit** sends the form. **After you submit, you can't change it.**
- If a form has expired or been cancelled, the page tells you so. Ask PnC to
  send it again.

## My Reviews (`/my-reviews`)

Where you follow your own **confirmation** (end of probation) and
**appraisal** reviews and, if you're a manager, your team's.

### My reviews

Each review shows its type, period, due date and your next step, for example:

- *Nominate your peers and fill in your self-reflection*
- *Your peers and manager are responding*
- *Everything is in; the meeting is being set up*
- *Meeting booked*
- *With PnC*
- *Completed*

You can see **who has responded, but not what they wrote.** You won't see
your manager's review or the decision on screen either. PnC will get in touch
with the outcome.

**Your part in a review:**

1. Open the review.
2. **Nominate peers.** Search with *Add a colleague*, add 4–5 people (not
   yourself, not your manager), then click **Save nominations**.
3. Click **Fill in my self-reflection**. The form opens in My Forms; fill it
   in and submit.
4. Your peers get their forms once your self-reflection is in. **After that
   your nominations are locked.** To add someone, ask PnC.
5. Go to the meeting. Once it's booked, you can move it from the review page
   if you need to.

The full process is in [Confirmations, appraisals and reviews](07-reviews.md).

### My team's reviews (managers)

If people report to you, a second list shows their reviews. For each one you:

1. **Fill in the manager review** (the form is in My Forms).
2. Read their self-reflection and peer feedback. Peer feedback appears
   **without names**, and only once at least two peers have answered.
3. Hold the review meeting. The platform books it automatically, and you can
   move it.
4. **Record the decision** after the meeting, with a note on what was
   discussed. It goes to PnC to accept.

## My Team (`/my-team`)

Appears once at least one person reports to you.

- Tiles: **Direct Reports**, **Confirmed / Active**, **Probation Reviews**.
- A list of your reportees with role, type, department, joining date and
  status. Search, and filter by *Active* or *Probation*.

> The *Confirmations* and *New Confirmation* buttons on this page only work
> for roles that can open Confirmations. If they send you back to the
> Dashboard, work from **My Reviews** instead.
